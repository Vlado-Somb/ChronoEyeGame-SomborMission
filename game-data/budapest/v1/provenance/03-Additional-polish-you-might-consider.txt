Additional polish you might consider
Cinematic transitionPlay a very brief full-screen “flash” or quick zoom-in before revealing the HUD.
HUD intro animationSlide the GPS panel, compass or minimap in from the side with a small easing curve.
Dynamic music layeringInstead of a hard cut, build up an additional percussion loop or synth pad as the player moves.
Contextual hintsShow a one-time tooltip near the HUD—“Tap a waypoint to lock on”—that fades out after they tap it.
Haptics or controller rumbleA short pulse when the mission starts, or when you spawn the first waypoint.
Environmental audioAdd distant city or nature sounds to remind them this is AR in a real place.
Objective trackerA small on-screen list “1. Find the embassy → 2. Decipher the plaque → 3. Speak to Tesla” that highlights the current step.
