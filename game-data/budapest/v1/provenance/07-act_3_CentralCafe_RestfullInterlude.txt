Act III: Centrál Grand Café & Bar - A Restful Interlude
Dialogues
Opening Monologue (Narrator):
"In this place, ideas brewed like storms in a teacup—wild, unrelenting, and forever changing the world outside its walls. The Centrál Grand Café & Bar was more than a haven for poets and revolutionaries; it was a crucible where thoughts took shape and dreams were forged. Inspector, pause here, for even the most relentless journeys need moments of reflection."
"This is where minds collided and souls intertwined, where words were exchanged that would echo across centuries. Take a moment to listen to the whispers of history still alive in the creak of chairs, the scent of old wood, and the ghostly clink of coffee cups long forgotten. Here, you might find the strength to continue your mission or the questions that will challenge its very purpose."
Narrator (Upon Scanning a Table):
"This table, Inspector, was once a battlefield of ideas. Imagine voices raised in spirited debate, dreams of freedom laid bare, and alliances forged over spilt coffee and laughter. Do you believe, Inspector, that revolutions begin in whispers, or in shouts?"
Player Response Options:
If the player answers whispers:
"Then you understand the power of the unseen—the ripple before the wave, the spark before the fire. Take this knowledge forward, for subtlety can shape destinies."
If the player answers shouts:
"True, shouts are what the world hears, but they are only the culmination. Behind every roar lies a murmur, behind every uprising, a quiet resolve. Remember this, Inspector."
Tesla’s Subtle Whisper:
A faint, distorted voice echoes through the AR device, almost imperceptible but resonant.
"Even in rest, the current flows. The greatest ideas are born not in haste but in stillness."
