Act I: Echoes of the Past
Dialogues
Opening Monologue (Narrator):
"You stand before the gates of the Serbian Embassy, a place where stories linger in the cracks of the stone and whispers of time echo against the walls. Here, the boundaries between the present and the past begin to blur. What was once a home for diplomacy and poetry now serves as a threshold to truths long forgotten, waiting to be uncovered by those who dare to look deeper."
"This is no ordinary place, Inspector. Dučić’s verses are not just words—they are keys, unlocking doorways to ideas beyond comprehension. As you step forward, you do not merely walk—you traverse the hidden veins of history, threading through moments where unity was forged and where shadows first fell over the truth."
"Jovan Dučić, a poet, a diplomat, a dreamer who danced between worlds. He spoke to nations in language that defied translation, words that bound the ephemeral to the eternal. His presence is no longer here, yet his essence lingers in the architecture, in the cadence of his verses, and in the silence that speaks his name. To stand here is to stand where he once imagined a future that we are yet to grasp."
Interaction with Dučić’s Legacy:
As the player discovers the engraved plaque near the gates, the narrator speaks:
"What you hold now is not just a fragment of art; it is a pulse of a nation, captured in lines and rhythms. Listen to it—feel its weight. Tell me, Inspector, do you believe that poetry holds power? Can words move mountains, or are they mere echoes against the immovable stones of history?"
Player Response Options:
If the player answers affirmatively:
"Then you are one of the few who understand. Dučić himself believed that words, like rivers, could carve through stone given time and intent. He envisioned poetry as an architecture of thought, not bound by mortar or brick, but by the will of those who believe. Look deeper, and you may find traces of his spirit still moving here."
If the player answers negatively:
"Ah, skepticism—a necessary shield in a world of distortion. But even shields can block the light. Dučić would tell you that a nation’s soul cannot be measured by doubt but by the daring to dream. Stay vigilant, Inspector, for sometimes, even echoes can carry the weight of truth."
Tesla’s Subtle Influence:
As the player scans a symbol engraved on the facade, faint energy ripples across their device. A distorted whisper brushes the edge of hearing:
"The current flows, unseen but unbroken. What binds us cannot be severed."
Transition to Act II:
"You have felt the pulse of this place, but the journey has only begun. The embassy was the first ripple in a greater current. Ahead lies a path lit by fragments of light and shadows of interference. The next step is yours to take—toward Hero’s Square, where monuments stand as sentinels of unity and conflict. Truth calls, Inspector. Do not let it wait."

Scene Description:

"The embassy was a beginning—a fragment of the whole. As you step into the shadows of Hero’s Square, remember: history is not a single thread but a tapestry stretched across time, fragile and waiting for your hand to mend it."

The path to Hero’s Square glows faintly, marked by holographic fragments of historical maps that dissolve as the player moves forward.
Forks
Fork 1: Scanning vs. Exploring
If the player chooses to scan the QR code on the gate: They are led directly to a digital archive containing Dučić’s poetry, unlocking the key verse from Ave Serbia.
"Poezija nije samo reči na papiru. To je šapat duše naroda." (Poetry is not just words on paper. It is the whisper of a nation’s soul.)
If the player manually explores the facade: They discover engraved verses and symbols leading to the same clue but feel rewarded for their attention to detail.
"Ovaj kamen pamti. Da li ćeš ti?" (This stone remembers. Will you?)
Riddles and Clues
Engraved Inscription Puzzle:
Players locate a weathered stone plaque near the embassy gates.
Inscription: "Traži ono što je skriveno u pesmi." (Seek what is hidden within the poem.)
Clue directs them to the QR code that links to Ave Serbia.
Key Verse from Ave Serbia:
"O, Srbijo, ti si san tvojih sinova, večna..." (O, Serbia, you are the eternal dream of your sons, eternal…)
Players must match this verse with a symbol carved into the embassy gate to progress.
