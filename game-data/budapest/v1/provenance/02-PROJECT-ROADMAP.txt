Focus: The overarching plot and narrative are broken into the game’s acts, along with character-driven moments and historical insights.
Subfolders:
Act_0_EmailApp_Introduction: Introducing players to the dystopian future, gameplay mechanics, and setting the stage (Ref: [218†source]).
Act_1_SerbianEmbassy_AnalogBeginning: Where players interact with Dučić’s legacy and unravel poetry-driven puzzles (Ref: [220†source]).
Act_2_HeroesSquare_GuardiansOfUnity: Tesla's battle with rival factions amidst historical statues, with themes of unity and memory (Ref: [221†source]).
Act_3_CentralCafe_RestfulInterlude: A reflective pause, introducing cultural immersion and intellectual discussions at Centrál Café (Ref: [217†source]).

Act_5_BudimHill_CulminationOfTruth: Players reconstruct a destroyed Serbian church, face distortions, and ensure history’s survival (Ref: [222†source]).

3. Technical Details and Unity C# Scripts
Focus: Development of game mechanics, Unity setups, and script debugging.
Subfolders:
Scripts: Organized by functionality:
ActScripts: C# scripts specific to triggering scenes and actions in Acts 0-5.
GPS: Code handling GPS interactions and object placement (Ref: Unified GPS Script).
UI: Interfaces for AR scanning and progress displays.
Unity_Configurations: AR Foundation setups, scene optimizations, and configurations for cross-platform builds.
Debug_Logs: Issues encountered and resolutions applied during development.
