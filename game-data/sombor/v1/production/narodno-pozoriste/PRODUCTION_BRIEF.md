# CHRONO EYE — NPS: Izgubljeni znak

**Mission:** sombor-nps / SO_NPS. **Status:** editorial prototype, not runtime tested.

## Verified history
The first performance in the building took place on 25 November 1882. The permanent professional company began in 1946; the institution adopted the National Theatre name in 1952. The theatre website gives 1879 for the shareholder society, while the city profile gives 1877. Do not quiz the disputed year.

Sources: official theatre "O nama"; Tourism Organisation of Vojvodina; Sombor community profile.

## Dramatic arc
A mysterious *imaginary* bell rings three times, but the curtain remains closed. Professor Vlada asks the player to become stage manager. The player must place CURTAIN behind the stage, ACTOR at center and AUDIENCE in front. After three correct placements, the curtain rises and the professor reveals the history. The player then answers the existing 1882 year question and the existing multiple-choice question: Narodno pozoriste Sombor, index 1.
